Features

Ek system, poora campus

Attendance se lekar principal reports tak — jo teacher ka time bachata hai aur student ko accountable banata hai.

Rotating-QR self-marking

Code har 30 sec badalta hai — screenshot share karke proxy lagana impossible. QR scan = session auto-fill.

Geo-fence & proxy detection

GPS boundary ke bahar se mark nahi hoga. Face recognition aur device checks se duplicate entries pakdi jati hain.

Live dashboards & heatmaps

Class-wise, subject-wise, student-wise — sab live. Defaulter list ek click me, threshold ke saath auto-flag.

Marks entry + Excel import

MST, quiz, assignment scores — template download karo, Excel me bharo, import karo. SPR me waise hi dikhte hain.

DWR & SPR auto-reports

Har lecture ka Daily Work Report 1 min me — 4:30 PM pe sab auto-compile ho kar Principal ke paas. SPR bhi ready-made.

Parent portal & certificates

Parents ko live attendance dikhta hai. Monthly register, bonafide aur character certificates — system se hi print.

How it works

3 steps. 1 minute.

1
Teacher class load karta hai

Program, semester, subject chuno — students ki roster turant aa jati hai. Keyboard se P/L/A ya voice commands.

2
Students QR scan karte hain

Rotating code 30 sec me refresh — ya teacher manually tap karta hai. Geo-fence ensure karta hai sab classroom me hain.

3
Reports khud ban jate hain

Dashboards, DWR, SPR, defaulter lists, parent alerts — sab automatic. Save ka button dabao aur bhool jao.
